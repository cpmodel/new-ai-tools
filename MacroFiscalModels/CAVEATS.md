- **Caveats:** (1) Return on public capital, public investment, constrained share, grants, net foreign debt and the tax split are income-group assumptions, flagged as such. ## 2026-10-20 - Integrated run: dashboard, NMIM, PriceFeasibility, GIMF / DIGNAD / DIGNAR-lite, LIC-DSF-lite
- **Task:** Bring the tools together (CPAT excluded): the dashboard drives a chain through the investment needs model, the pricing feasibility metric and the macro and debt models, and shows the results; pilots (KEN, SLE, UGA, LBR) then all countries.
- **Inputs:** Dashboard panel (now with Appendix C: WEO revenue, primary balance, investment, current account, 5-year growth); NMIM v0.2 engine; `PriceFeasibility/output/country_metrics.csv` (default thresholds, hybrid mix); WDI fossil rents.
- **Outputs:** `integrated.py`, `pricing_bridge.py`; `nmim_bridge.programme` splits the budget need into capital and spending and returns NMIM's net zero power CO2 (TODO item 7 done); GIMF-lite `g_extra`, `rev_extra`; DIGNAD-lite `prog_spend`, `rev` and the DIGNAR-lite resource block; `calibration.to_dignar` and DIGNAR routing; `results/integrated_results.csv` (186 countries: 135 GIMF-lite, 35 DIGNAD-lite, 16 DIGNAR-lite; 184 solved, 15 of them with a tightened fiscal rule, 2 not financeable); dashboard Appendix C and D (64 indicators, 11 blocks; Python and R pipelines, config, workbook texts, README, SETUP, MANIFEST entries for changed files); user guide 4; method note sections 2.3 and 4b; deck numbers refreshed (Kenya: +4.8% GDP by 2050, 84% from energy savings).
- **Caveats:** (1) Splitting spending from capital lowers the public part's GDP effect sharply (Kenya 2050: 1.85% to 0.26% in DIGNAD-lite with the shared calibration); about 80% of Kenya's and 97% of Liberia's 2030 budget need is land-use and non-CO2 spending. (2) Energy savings enter GIMF-lite only, so GIMF-lite countries show larger gains (median +7.7% GDP by 2050 against +0.7% for DIGNAD-lite); gains above 20% (Palau, Lebanon, Kuwait, Egypt) are driven by savings. (3) Fossil rents fall 80% by 2050 in DIGNAR-lite (median -14% GDP by 2050), a global transition effect, not the programme's. (4) Pricing revenue is power-only; it exceeds the budget need in some oil-fired small islands (Jamaica 5.5 times). (5) DSF baseline uses defaults for the external share of debt, exports and capacity; 5 of 66 low and lower-middle income ratings worsen with the programme (Cameroon, DR Congo, Liberia, Madagascar, Chad). (6) R pipeline additions are unverified (R not available). (7) The panel was rebuilt on Linux: values are unchanged, but the long files' source column now holds file names instead of Windows paths; the MANIFEST was already stale for other files (line endings) and only the changed files' entries were updated. (8) `Global_fiscal_indicators.xlsx` is generated and not committed, as before. (9) Data issues: TODO item 17.
