# CPAT-AI-Mitigation-MVP (mitigation module, copy-pasteable)

Replacement for the CPAT mitigation module whose formulas are **fully copy-pasteable**: one formula per block, valid across (years) and down (subsectors x fuels), and a whole scenario group can be copied to a new scenario. Goal: auditable equations that a model builder can extend by dragging.

Current version: `CPAT-AI-Mitigation-MVP-v2.3.xlsm` (macro-enabled; v2.3: last year 2041 (legacy horizon) as the LAMBDA year, every calculated cell of 2041 calls its named LAMBDA (Excel 365), 2022-2040 plain formulas; fuel prices for power as legacy (centred 5-year moving average, switch SpotPow); v2.2: one version number for every current file, quick start and repository guide in `QuickStart_v2_3.md` and at the start of the Word documentation, one register of bugs, issues and upgrades; existing carbon pricing from the legacy ECP data (sheet `ECP`, MTInputs 208-214) and power in the main thread: generation fuel use in the fuel totals, power revenues in the revenue totals, check rows `ener.chk`, `rev.chk`, `co2.chk` (v1.09); works without LAMBDA support: plain formulas in every cell, the named LAMBDAs an optional library on sheet `Lambdas` (v1.08); every cell formula at most 100 characters, planned retirement by generation type, electricity demand memo lines, power indicators and charts (v1.07); power engineer model with demand, dispatch, retirement and investment, power CO2 in section 13, ten legacy bugs fixed (v1.06, `PowerModel_Method_v2_3.md`, report `CPAT-AI-Mitigation-MVP_BugsIssuesUpgrades_v2.3.docx`); power generation costs and electricity prices in section 3 (v1.05, `PowerPrices_Method_v2_3.md`); first-run test macro CheckBatchRun with sheet MacroCheck (v1.04); renamed from CPAT_Mitigation_CopyPaste at v1.00; multiple scenarios through scenario definitions, a VBA batch run, StoredResults and ScenarioCompare (v1.03, `Scenarios_Method_v2_3.md`); policies incl. a cap-based new ETS with benchmarks (v1.02, `ETS_Method_v2_3.md`) and feebates with sectoral shadow prices, domestic prices with existing taxes and subsidies, price -> fuel use, revenues, CO2 emissions, 2022-2040). Earlier versions in `Old/`. Price method, existing carbon pricing and the explicit real/nominal assumptions: `PriceProjection_Method_v2_3.md`. Differences with legacy CPAT: workbook sheet `LegacyDiff`. Data changed by assumption (other oil products, VAT rate) is bright yellow with red text in `Inputs_prices`.

## Design (agreed)

Layout follows the legacy CPAT Mitigation sheet: numbered sections **1. Policies**, **2. Retail energy prices**, **3. Power sector** (engineer model, generation costs and electricity prices, v1.05-v1.07), **5. Transport**, **6. Buildings**, **7. Industrial**, **8. Other energy use**, **11. Results - energy consumption** (subsectors and power generation), **12. Revenues** (subsectors and power), **13. Emissions** (subsectors and power).

| Dimension | Axis | Nest |
|---|---|---|
| Scenario | columns | outer: one group per scenario (output-code column, base year 2022 - column L for scenario 1 as in legacy - and 2023-2041 (2041 = the LAMBDA year), then a blank spacer column) |
| Year | columns | inner |
| Sector section | rows | outer (sections 5-8), each with a total fuel-use line |
| Subsector | rows | heading line (its total fuel use), the 16 CPAT energy-use subsectors |
| Variable | rows | `ctxnew` new carbon tax, `ets` new ETS (tax-equivalent, auctioned part), `ntx` new excise (fuel price reform), `nce` total new policy, `xcp` existing carbon pricing, `xcpd` its change against the price data, `atp` after-tax price, `shp` shadow price on the efficiency margin, `ener` fuel use, `co2`, revenues `rtx`, `rsub`, `rnew`, `rxcp` |
| Fuel | rows | inner: coa nga gso die lpg ker oop bio |

- **Section 1 (policies)** reads the scenario inputs from `MTInputs` (one *Used for calculation* column per scenario from column J; rows A:H = legacy template) by MTInputs row (hidden column D) and scenario number (row 5): carbon tax (`CPIntro`..`CPOutro`, `ExtendCarbonPriceBeyondOutro`), carbon-tax coverage by fuel and sector (`MCov*`), fuel price reform (rows 140-169), feebates (rows 53-64, coverage 66-82). It computes the carbon price (legacy rule), the fuel price reform paths (14 price fuels) and the feebate rate paths (power, transport, residential, industry). One default for all paths: 0 before the start year, linear to the target, continuing linearly afterwards (the carbon price keeps its MTInputs switch). Shadow prices: by sector ($/tCO2, legacy rows 2345-2349) = feebate path (later + non-auctioned ETS, regulations); share impacting efficiency by subsector (`ssc`, legacy rows 2403-2419) = feebate coverage x adjustment (1.0 for feebates).
- **Real terms (v0.9):** everything is in real USD of `ResultsYear` (2026) per GJ. Row `infl` (US CPI index) converts nominal domestic data and nominal policy inputs; row `defl` (US GDP deflator index) converts international prices. The two differ by up to 1.2% before 2030, so both are kept. MTInputs policy values are real, except a carbon price marked `NomorReal` = Nominal. Values after 2030 are not a legacy target.
- **Section 2 (prices, by 12 price fuels):** `gp` international prices (MTInputs source and High/Low adjustment; country gas market); `sp` = data up to 2024, then fixsp + floating part x gp(t)/gp(t-1); `txo` = data, then the legacy pass-through rule; `rpb` = (sp + txo) x (1 + VAT rate). VAT rate: dataset where filled, else VAT_WEO (Egypt 14%) for residential coal/gas and oil products, 0 for power, industry, biomass (v0.10 assumption). Data step: sheet `Inputs_prices`.
- **Wedges:** `ctxnew` = carbon price x EF x fuel coverage x sector coverage x (1 - ETS coverage); `ets` = ETS tax-equivalent price x EF x effective ETS coverage x (1 - OBR share); `ntx` = fuel price reform path / GJ per price unit; `nce` = `ctxnew` + `ets` + `ntx`; `atp` = max(`rpb` of the price fuel + (`nce` + `xcpd`) x (1 + VAT rate), 0.01); `shp` = (sector shadow price x `ssc` + ETS tax-equivalent price x ETS coverage x OBR share) x EF.
- **ETS (v1.02, `ETS_Method_v2_3.md`):** MTInputs rows 84-115 per scenario; replaces the carbon tax in covered sectors. Cap = baseline covered emissions (data rows `bco2` = scenario 1's CO2 by ETS sector, refreshed by the build) x (1 + change path). Price = fast estimate LN(cap/baseline) / (effective semi-elasticity x volatility adjustment), or the override row `ets.ovr` (MTInputs `D_ETSPriceOverride` = Yes; filled by `ets_goalseek_v2_3.py` or by pasting `ets.next`). Benchmarks by sector group give the OBR share; the price splits into a tax-equivalent wedge in `atp` (auctioned part) and a shadow price on the efficiency margin (OBR part). Volatility adjustment from MTInputs 95-97 (1/1.1 at Medium). Section 13 compares covered emissions with the cap. Sectoral shadow prices = feebates + OBR part of the ETS + regulations (placeholder 0).
- **Existing carbon pricing (v1.09, `PriceProjection_Method_v2_3.md` section 5b):** sheet `ECP` holds the legacy existing carbon tax and ETS data (`extract_ecp_v2_3.py`: prices by year, sector and fuel coverage; 52 countries, none for Egypt) and the values of the country in Settings C4 (real prices; coverage of the last data year). Section 1 reads MTInputs 208-214 (`ExistingCTApply`, `ExistingCTGrowth`, `ExistingETSApply`, `ExistingETSGrowth`, `CTaxComplimentaryToETS`): price used = data x switch, growing after the last data year; level embedded in the price data. Per subsector and fuel `xcp` (level) and `xcpd` (change against the price data, in `atp` and `rtx`); power `xck` in the variable cost; revenue memo `rxcp`. Complementary switch: the new carbon tax covers 1 - existing ETS sector coverage.
- **Power prices (v1.05, `PowerPrices_Method_v2_3.md`):** section 3 keeps legacy's sub-tables. A inputs: capex time factor `tcf`, storage cost paths (generation shares, investment shares and consumption come from the engineer model since v1.06). B: `cax`, vintage averages `caxav`, `stoav` (last year's investment share), amortised fixed cost `fix` = caxav x acap + afix + stoav (legacy B3-B8 in one row). C: levelised fixed cost `lfx` of new plants (investment only). D: fuel price for power `fpp` = centred 5-year moving average of the section-2 price before new policies, as legacy A18 (Settings C21 SpotPow = Yes: spot; v2.3); variable cost `vbc` = O&M + `fpp` x 0.0036/efficiency + change in existing carbon pricing `xck` (v1.09); new policy cost per kWh `ccp` (carbon tax, ETS, fuel price reform on power fuels). E: generation cost `gnc` = fix + vbc, averages `gncav` and `ccpav`. G (residential, industry): supply cost `sc` = gncav of the previous year + T&D; price before new policies `rppre` = data to 2024, then + pass-through x change in sc x (1 + VAT); end-user price `rp` = rppre + ccpav; subsidy gap `sgap`; revenues `rvat`, `rnvat` (VAT on the policy cost passed through), `rgap`, memo `rcarb` (USD million). M (v1.09): revenues by type `prtx`, `prsub` (fuel taxes and subsidies on generation), `prxc` (change in existing carbon pricing), `prnew` (new policies, ETS at the permit price), `prxl` (existing carbon pricing level); they form the power rows of section 12. J: VRE share `vre`, storage cost per hour `cph`, marginal storage cost `msc`. Constants per type in sheet `Inputs_power` (from data sheets `PowerTech`, `PowerPaths`, `PowerParams`, `PowerDemand`, written by `extract_power_v2_3.py`). The method reproduces the cached legacy rows exactly (check 4b).
- **Power engineer model (v1.06-v1.09, `PowerModel_Method_v2_3.md`):** section 3 continues with the legacy sub-tables H-L, year by year as legacy. H: electricity demand by subsector (15) grows with a multiple per demand group (efficiency, GDP with the income factor, last year's electricity price); required generation = demand x balance factor. I: fixed-output types at their capacity factor, coal and gas by minima and a logit on variable cost; shares, fuel use, CO2. K: capacity carried forward, retirements, cost-based early coal retirement, capacity needed. L: investment by a logit on the levelised investment cost (C + D9) within scale-up limits, then cheapest remaining space; nameplate and USD investment; phi for the vintages. D6 shadow price and rebate, D9 levelised variable cost (look-ahead rows `dsum`, `lvc`: plain formulas), E1. Generation shares, investment shares and consumption are modelled (the v1.05 interim data are gone). Power is in the main thread: CO2 in section 13 (total, by fuel, ETS sector power), generation fuel use of coal, gas, oil and biomass in section 11 (row `ener.pow`, totals by fuel; v1.09), revenues in section 12 (rows `.pow`; v1.09). Aligned with legacy except ten bugs: `power_model_ref_v2_3.py` (FIXES) reproduces 208 legacy rows of the baseline and the carbon-tax run with the bugs on; `CPAT-AI-Mitigation-MVP_BugsIssuesUpgrades_v2.3.docx` explains each bug and its effect. Settings C19 switches the legacy 2023-2024 demand shock on (default off, as the fuel blocks; the resulting 2023 demand jump is accepted, user decision v1.07). Planned retirement (v1.07): PowerTech `planned_retirement` = Yes makes a type retire the data of PowerPaths `pret|<type>` (nameplate MW) instead of capacity x 1/lifetime (Egypt: no data, all No). Memo lines `eldem` (v1.07): electricity demand by subsector in each sector block and by sector in section 11, not in the fuel totals (they count the fuels used to generate it); sector policies do not act on power demand (user decision v1.07).
- **Existing taxes and subsidies (v0.11, section 2):** `vat`, `etx` = max(txo, 0), `esub` = max(-txo, 0), `esubpu` = subsidy per MTInputs price unit (input for a later per-fuel policy). 
- **Multiple scenarios (v1.03, `Scenarios_Method_v2_3.md`):** MTInputs J = baseline, K = live policy scenario (Mitigation group 2), L onwards = scenario definitions (row 4 Run? = Yes/No; shipped: $20 carbon price, Package A carbon tax + feebates, Package B ETS + carbon tax elsewhere). Macro `RunAllScenarios` (module CPATScenarios, Alt+F8) stores the baseline once, then copies each definition into K, recalculates, runs the ETS goal seek when the definition applies a new ETS, and stores the MTOutputs block as values in `StoredResults`; K is restored. `ScenarioCompare` shows levels, differences and % differences vs the baseline for one year (C4) and up to 8 stored IDs. Other macros: StoreBaseline, StoreLiveScenario, SolveETSLive, ClearStoredScenarios, and CheckBatchRun (v1.04): the first-run test for Excel, reported on sheet `MacroCheck` (see `Scenarios_Method_v2_3.md`, First run in Excel).
- **MTOutputs and Charts (v1.01):** `MTOutputs` collects 58 key results (v1.09: power fuel use and revenues, existing carbon pricing revenue and prices; v1.07: 18 power indicators: demand total and by sector, required generation, capacity, wind and solar capacity, investment, generation shares, average policy cost, power carbon revenue) (fuel use, CO2, covered CO2 and ETS cap, revenues, carbon and ETS price, key retail prices) for every scenario by output code: one row block per scenario, the scenario number in column E of the block header. Copy the last block below to add a scenario. `Charts` has 10 line charts (CO2, fuel use incl. power generation, fiscal effect, carbon price; v1.07: power generation, power CO2, VRE share, residential electricity price, capacity, electricity demand), one series per scenario.
- **Revenues (v0.12, v1.09):** three separate calculations per subsector and fuel (USD million real): `rtx` existing tax revenue = fuel use x PJ/ktoe x (`etx` + `vat` + `xcpd` x (1 + VAT rate)); `rsub` existing subsidy cost = fuel use x PJ/ktoe x `esub`; `rnew` new-policy revenue = fuel use x PJ/ktoe x (`ctxnew` + `ntx` + `ets` x revenue factor + `nce` x VAT rate). `rxcp` existing carbon pricing (memo). Section 12: totals by sector and power (`.pow`: fuel taxes and subsidies on generation, change in existing carbon pricing, electricity VAT and subsidy gap, new policies with the ETS at the permit price), net existing (`rnet`), total (`rtot`), change vs scenario 1 (`rtot.chg`), check `rev.chk`. Feebates revenue-neutral.
- **CO2 (v0.13):** `co2` per subsector and fuel = fuel use x PJ/ktoe x EF (tCO2/GJ) in MtCO2; section 13: total, by sector, power, by fuel, change vs scenario 1, check `co2.chk`. Fuel combustion only (no process emissions, CH4, N2O, local pollutants yet).
- **Data step separate from formulas.** Sheet `Inputs` (one row per fuel|subsector) holds every lookup; `Mitigation` reads it in the hidden parameter columns D:G (labels in `Variables` G:J) and the base-year column. Calculation cells hold no searching lookups; `ctxnew`, `ntx` and `shp` pick a Section-1 row by position with `INDEX(range, position)`.
- **Short formulas (v1.07, NORMS section 3):** every cell formula has at most 100 characters (checked). Longer logic sits in the named LAMBDAs; data lookups use defined names for the data tables (`WEOVal`, `PDomVal`, `PPathVal`, `PTechVal`, `VarKey`, ...); multi-step formulas show their steps in helper rows (section 1: `cp.t`, `cp.nom`, `ets.on`, `ets.vol`, `ets.imp`, `ets.chg`, policy rates by sector `ctr`, `etr`, `shpx`; section 2: `gp.src`, `gp.adj`, `gpn`, `txf`; section 3: `ecmin`, `ecshr`; section 13: `ets.lr`) or helper columns (Inputs_prices by year, MTOutputs, ScenarioCompare). Fuel-use parameters: G = AEEI factor (1/(1+alpha))^(1+eps_U), F = efficiency exponent eps_F x (1+eps_U) (Inputs). Column H reads the member labels (sector | fuel) from a table on Mapping (v1.08).
- **Copy-pasteable; LAMBDA year 2041 (v1.08, v2.3):** every variable has one formula (relative R1C1) per column block across all subsectors, fuels and scenarios for 2022-2040, and the last year 2041 calls the named LAMBDA of the row (user decision v2.3: the LAMBDAs as a full year of the flow). 2022-2040 calculate in any Excel from 2019 (MINIFS, AGGREGATE) and in LibreOffice (the check recalculates there: errors only in 2041; the power look-ahead then uses last year's values for 2041); 2041 needs Excel 365. Supply cost and taxes have separate history (2022-2024, data) and projection blocks without IF. Sheet `Lambdas` gives, for every calculated row, its LAMBDA formula (88 named LAMBDAs; it holds the IF between history and projection where needed); the check copies each over its whole row and gets the same results.
- **Codes and labels.** Column A holds the variable code; description, unit and source come from `Variables`; each scenario group starts with a code column building `country.mit.<variable>.<subsector>.<fuel>.<suffix>.<scenario>` (e.g. `egy.mit.ener.rod.gso.e.1`).
- **Roll-up (v0.15).** Mitigation is the first tab and opens at 75% zoom, fully rolled up (bands, sector totals, carbon price, retail prices, revenue and CO2 totals). Row group buttons sit below each group (summaryBelow). Columns are grouped at one level: B:G, I:K and 2030-2039 of each scenario. Each scenario group ends with a blank spacer column. Colours: darker beige = base year; light beige = blocks copy-pasteable within themselves but not draggable into white or base-year cells (LAMBDA column, 2023-2024 price history). Green section bands have white text.
- **Parameters are global**; scenarios differ only in their MTInputs columns. Country lookups via `Settings!C4` (elasticities and prices for all CPAT countries; energy use, GDP and EFs Egypt only).

## Add a scenario

**Batch (v1.03, recommended):** copy the last scenario definition on `MTInputs` (column N) one column to the right, edit its inputs and name (row 6), set row 4 Run? = Yes, then run the macro `RunAllScenarios` (enable macros; Alt+F8). Results are stored as values in `StoredResults` and compared in `ScenarioCompare`.
