# Version notes: Integrated MF-C tool

The shipped workbook is `Integrated_MFC_Tool_v<version>.xlsx` in this folder, built by
`build/build_mfc_tool.py`; the version is set in `build/config/tool.json`. To ship a new version:
bump `version`, move the previous workbook to `Archive/`, rebuild, and add a line here.

| Version | Date | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| 0.1 | 2026-10-20 | `Archive/Integrated_MFC_Tool_v0.1.xlsx`. First version: the Global Fiscal Indicators dashboard (v1.2 panel, 65 indicators, 11 blocks) plus the investment needs module, two blocks after Context 3: Investment 1 (v1.1 workbook, 2030: total and incremental need, incremental need by 7 sectors, incremental cost under lenses a/b/c, cost per tonne, cost-of-capital penalty, PIMA-adjusted need; 194 economies) and Investment 2 (NMIM v0.2: net zero investment by who pays P1-P5, direct budget need, private share, incremental investment 2030 and 2050, PV of incremental cost; 186 economies). 217 countries, 91 indicators, 13 blocks |
| 0.2 | 2026-10-20 | `Archive/Integrated_MFC_Tool_v0.2.xlsx`. Investment needs from NMIM v0.2 only (v1.1 workbook dropped from the tool). Investment 1, need and cost: net zero and incremental investment, incremental by 7 sectors, 2050, incremental cost lenses a/b/c, cost-of-capital penalty, cost per tonne, abatement, PV 2025-2050. Investment 2, public investment needs: direct budget need with P1 and P4-gap parts, incremental and PIMA-adjusted budget need, plus regulated utilities, all investment needing public money, private capital with P3/P5/P4 parts, private share. 186 economies; 217 countries, 94 indicators, 13 blocks |
| 0.3 | 2026-10-20 | Macro-fiscal module. Macro 1 (financing gap after feasible power carbon-pricing revenue, pricing share of the need, feasible carbon tax, abatement) and Macro 2 (GDP vs today's path 2030/2050, GDP vs baseline 2050, debt 2030/2050, debt added by the programme, LIC-DSF-lite risk before and with the programme) from the MacroFiscalModels integrated run; they replace the dashboard's Appendix D. New Macro sheet: the integrated run's GDP and debt paths beside DIGNAD-R, the recursive DIGNAD-lite / DIGNAR-lite in live formulas for the Dashboard's country (186 countries), with charts, plus Macro_assumptions, Macro_model and Macro_inputs. 217 countries, 102 indicators, 14 blocks |
| 0.4 | 2026-10-20 | Dashboard v1.3 panel: model and DSF inputs in Appendix C, new Appendix E fiscal risk (Climate Fiscal Risk Dashboard v1.5). Macro 2 adds the other model family as a cross-check (GDP and debt 2050) and the LIC-DSF Composite Indicator; the integrated run behind it now uses dashboard public investment, CPIA-based debt-carrying capacity and the IDS external share. New DSA sheet (five LIC-DSF-lite indicators against thresholds, ratings, charts); Macro sheet adds the cross-check rows and lines. New Appendix F: CDPM-method cross-check from the v1.1 workbook. 217 countries, 124 indicators, 16 blocks. v0.3 in `Archive/` |
| 0.5 | 2026-10-20 | New front page, Fiscal outlook: country picker (the Dashboard follows it), levers for new taxes (pp of GDP a year, ceiling, share on capital), new public investment and other spending (start, ramp-up), fiscal rule strength, and power-sector carbon pricing; revenue and expenditure by item, balance, debt, growth vs trend and green public investment to 2050 for the policy scenario and the no-new-measures baseline, with charts. New Pricing sheet: PriceFeasibility v0.1 in formulas, tax-by-feebate feasibility grid, live best feasible tax, feebate and hybrid under the F1-F3 limits, revenue use (bottom-40% rebate, green public investment, budget). DIGNAD-R's policy scenario takes the levers; the carbon revenue replaces the fixed pricing path (p_pricing retired). Build paths fixed after the folder move. v0.4 in `Archive/` |
| 0.6 | 2026-10-20 | New hierarchy. Dashboard (front page): country selector every tab follows, main levers (revenue increase, public investment increase, climate investment programme on/off, carbon pricing mode), headline results 2030/2050, six charts (debt, revenue and expenditure, GDP, green investment, carbon price in force, DSA). Detail tabs: Indicators (the dashboard's Dashboard, renamed), Fiscal outlook (compact: table and charts, with carbon tax and feebate rates in force), Macro & debt (Macro and DSA merged), Green investment (NMIM v0.2 engine copied in live: GI_Inputs, GI_Model, GI_Data; the climate programme, power CO2 and GDP now feed the macro model live), Carbon pricing (renamed), Policy settings (secondary levers). Charts restyled: numeric year axis every five years, legend below, colours by role; DSA charts fixed (hidden data now plotted, thresholds carried across years). v0.5 in `Archive/` |
